- Fixed: "Due next week" left out next week's Extra Task even when ClickUp had already created it - next week (Sunday to Saturday) was looked up under the same key as today, so it was handed today's finished Extra Task. It now looks again after the close (and once more a minute and a half later, once ClickUp has created the new one), whenever you press ↻ / Refresh now, and within 5 minutes on its own.
- Fixed: right after the Extra Task was closed, the two "This week" cards could disagree (e.g. 48h 12m vs 46h 20m by due date). ClickUp can create next week's Extra Task starting on the day the old one closed, so it overlapped this week; the filter card counted that new one (and added a daily share for days it doesn't even cover). For a week, the occurrence due in that week now counts, and an Extra Task with dates only counts on its own days.
- Improved: after changing Multi-day tasks, the number cards dim with "Updating…" too, not just the task lists - today's card comes back as soon as today is rebuilt, the week cards when the week is.

Every request to ClickUp now goes through one guard: no more than 4 at a time, no more than 90 a minute (ClickUp allows 100), a pause when ClickUp says only a few are left this minute, and nothing sent while ClickUp has asked to wait - so a short wait is simply waited out instead of failing, and failed retries no longer stretch the limit.

The dashboard now recalculates a week older than 5 minutes when you open it (and only then, so re-opening it doesn't use up your ClickUp calls), the card's header shows when the week was last rebuilt and explains itself if that is over 30 minutes, and a day with an estimate but no tracked time yet shows the estimate in amber instead of "0m". - Improved: the floating timer window shows today's total in its small view, on the line under how the task is going - you no longer have to point at the window to see how much you have tracked today.
